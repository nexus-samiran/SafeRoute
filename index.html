About Safe Route
Safe Route is a women's safety mapping platform focused on major cities in India. It combines real-time area risk data with routing intelligence to show you not just the fastest route — but the safest one.

Data source: Supabase-backed area safety dataset with crowd density, lighting conditions, police presence, and historical incident data.

Routing: OSRM open routing engine with multi-alternative route scoring.
